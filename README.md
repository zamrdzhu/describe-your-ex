One word that describes an ex, written for make.fun's "Describe Your Ex in One Word" bounty.

## The word

> Unsubscribed

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one word and writes the same word (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this word

An ex is someone who cancelled the relationship the way you cancel a newsletter: one quiet click, no explanation, and suddenly the messages stop arriving. "Unsubscribed" is instantly readable as a verdict on a former partner, it lands the joke through a modern twist rather than a plain insult, and anyone who has ever been ghosted recognises the feeling at once — the relationship didn't end in a fight, it ended in an inbox. One word, twelve characters, well inside the 111-character gate. It needs no caption: the joke arrives with the word itself.
